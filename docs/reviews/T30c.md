## Review

1. `app/src/shared/i18n/messages/ranking.ts:50`, should-fix. The English `scopeNote` says "does not use … test results", which is not true. Once a lab finding is entered as an HPO term the ranking does use it, for example "Low cholesterol esterification rate" in demo-npc1 and CK/AST/ALT in demo-dmd. Suggest "test results that are not entered as features", or just "genetic test results", changed in all 6 languages together. The age/sex/ancestry list in this sentence is also beyond the requested sentence "phenotype overlap only".
2. `intake.ts:146` (ja) and `intake.ts:239` (ko), should-fix. `demoPrecision` translates "precise" as 正確 / 정확, which means "accurate". That reads as if the family's notes were inaccurate, stronger than the English. Suggest ja 「より詳しく具体的です」 and ko 「더 구체적이고 자세합니다」.
3. `trials.ts:13` and `TrialsPanel.tsx:78`, nit. The new sentence follows directly after `ledeWithCandidate`; both say "ask the doctor", and one uses "doctor" while the other uses "clinician". Use "the doctor" in both, or drop the asking part from the second sentence.
4. `trials.ts:87` (ja), nit. 「おすすめ」 is colloquial; 「推奨」 fits a medical context better.
5. `ranking.ts:329` / `:610` (ja/ko), nit. ancestry is translated 祖先 / 조상, literally "forebears". More natural: 「祖先の出身地域」 / 「혈통」.
