# Rare Disease Case Intelligence (T38)

Offline research data and tooling for Rare Disease Detective. It holds verified open-access case reports, verified HPO terms, a lay-language dictionary and an evaluation harness. Nothing here is used by the app, the engine or the functions, and no model is trained.

Spec: [`.kiro/specs/case-intelligence/`](../../.kiro/specs/case-intelligence/).
